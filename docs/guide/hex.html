      Hex

      The optional routes through Hex: irreducibility, determinants and primes in cas, factors over the rationals with every one certified, and primality.

      Hex is the Lean FRO’s verified computer algebra: dense polynomials over ℤ, ℚ and ZMod p, factoring with irreducibility certificates, determinants, primality and real roots. It works the way SymLean does, running its searches in compiled code and having the kernel check a certificate. And it is strong exactly where the core of SymLean is weak, in exact algebra over the integers and finite fields, so I’d much rather use Hex there than write a second version of the same algorithms.

      We’re in touch with the Hex team about how the two projects fit together.

      SymLean uses Hex through the optional library CASHex, and if you don’t ask for it, nothing changes: Lake neither fetches nor builds Hex, and no module of CAS imports it. To turn it on with Hex v0.10.0, run these from the root of SymLean:

      lake -R -Khex=on update HexBerlekampZassenhausTheory HexBareissTheory HexPrimalityTheory
git -C .lake/packages/HexLLL apply ../../../CASHex/patches/hex-lll-gnu-source.patch
lake -R -Khex=on build CASHex

      The first command fetches the Berlekamp-Zassenhaus factorizer, the Bareiss determinants and the primality certificates with their dependencies, 34 Hex packages in all, and writes them into lake-manifest.json. The second is for glibc systems that declare RTLD_DEFAULT only under _GNU_SOURCE. To switch back, run lake -R build, and git checkout lake-manifest.json restores the manifest.

      ModuleWhat it adds
      CASHex.Algebrathe Hex route of bare cas: irreducibility, determinants and primality
      CASHex.Factorirreducible factors over ℚ for factor, and the factorizer over ℚ of apart, product, rsolve, dsolve, inverseLaplace, eigenvalues, eigenvectors and rational integration
      CASHex.LinearAlgebrathe Hex determinant of det
      CASHex.NumberTheoryprimeQ, moebiusMu and factorInteger, and eulerPhi from the factorization

      In proofs

      • casProves the goal by computer algebra whose steps Lean’s kernel checks: identities, derivatives, integrals, limits and bounds.

      Factoring over the rationals

      • factorFactors a polynomial with rational coefficients, with a theorem that the product equals the input.
      • apartWrites a rational function of one variable as a polynomial plus partial fractions, with the conditions of the identity.
      • productA finite product in closed form, telescoped or in factorials, with a theorem for every upper bound.
      • eigenvaluesThe exact eigenvalues of a square matrix with rational entries, with their multiplicities and a theorem that there are no others.
      • eigenvectorsA basis of each eigenspace of a square matrix with rational entries, with a theorem for every eigenvalue, irrational ones included.

      Numbers and matrices

      • primeQWhether a natural number is prime, with a proof of Nat.Prime n or its negation, through the optional Hex library.
      • moebiusMuThe Möbius function μ(n) of a positive integer from its prime factorization, through the optional Hex library.
      • factorIntegerThe prime factorization of a natural number n≥2 with a theorem about Nat.primeFactorsList, through the optional Hex library.
      • eulerPhiEuler’s totient φ(n), the number of residues modulo n coprime to n, with a theorem about Mathlib’s Nat.totient.
      • detThe determinant of a square matrix with rational entries, with a theorem about Mathlib’s Matrix.det.

      Settings

      • OptionsThe settings of cas, cega and #cas: the budget of a call, what a report shows, and the trace classes.

      The examples below are quoted from CASHex/Examples.lean, which the Hex build compiles, and each one is checked by #guard_msgs or by its theorem. With import CASHex.Algebra, bare cas proves that the AES polynomial is irreducible over ZMod 2, by a Rabin certificate that the kernel checks:

      In[1]:=
      theorem irreducible_aes : Irreducible (X ^ 8 + X ^ 4 + X ^ 3 + X + 1 : (ZMod 2)[X]) := by
  cas

      The proof uses only the three standard axioms:

      In[2]:=
      #print axioms irreducible_aes

      Quoted from CASHex/Examples.lean, where #guard_msgs pins the output.

      Out[2]=

      CASHex.Examples.irreducible_aes depends on the axioms propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound.

      Factoring shows the difference best. Without Hex, #cas [factor] splits off rational roots, perfect powers and homogeneous factors, so it leaves x4+4 whole, although x4+4 does factor over ℚ. With import CASHex.Factor, the factors come with their irreducibility over ℚ, proved in the kernel, whenever Hex certifies every one of them:

      In[3]:=
      #cas [factor] (x ^ 4 + 4)

      Quoted from CASHex/Examples.lean, where #guard_msgs pins the output (the part shown).

      Out[3]=

      scalar ℝ, factors irreducible over ℚ

      (x2−2x+2)(x2+2x+2)
      (x^2 - 2*x + 2)*(x^2 + 2*x + 2)

      Primes work the same way. A prime is proved by Hex’s Pocklington certificate, which the kernel replays. This one is 261−1:

      #cas (primeQ 2305843009213693951)

      These routes are kernel-only, because an optional library shouldn’t be where the trusted base grows. A proof that uses an axiom beyond propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound that the goal and its hypotheses don’t already use is rejected, and so is a proof with a hole. Hex’s search runs under the budget cas.hexHeartbeats, and setting it to 0 turns the route off.
