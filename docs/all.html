      Index A to Z

      Every mode, function, tactic and option of SymLean, by name.

      A B C D E F G H I J L M N O P R S T Z

      A

      • apartWrites a rational function of one variable as a polynomial plus partial fractions, with the conditions of the identity.

      B

      • BernoulliBBernoulli numbers Bn and Bernoulli polynomials Bn(x) for n≤60, with theorems about Mathlib’s bernoulli and Polynomial.bernoulli.
      • BesselJProved decimal enclosures of Mathlib’s Bessel function Complex.besselJ at an integer order and a rational point.
      • binomialThe binomial coefficient (nk) of natural numbers n and k, with a theorem about Mathlib’s Nat.choose.

      C

      • cancelCancels common factors in each summand of a rational expression, with the conditions under which the result agrees.
      • #casThe calculator: evaluates a call, simplifies an expression or runs a mode, and proves its answer.
      • casProves the goal by computer algebra whose steps Lean’s kernel checks: identities, derivatives, integrals, limits and bounds.
      • cas [integrate primitive := F]Proves a definite integral from a primitive that you supply: the derivative is checked, then the fundamental theorem of calculus applies.
      • cas usingProves the goal as cas does, with your hypotheses as evidence for the conditions that the routes need.
      • cas?Proves the goal as cas does, and suggests the call to keep in the proof.
      • cas_nfRewrites the real terms of a goal or a hypothesis to a normal form, with a checked proof of each rewrite.
      • cegaProves numerical bounds: inequalities on a box of real variables, closed constants and interval integrals.
      • cega usingProves a goal from a certificate that you built once, by the certificate’s own soundness theorem.
      • cega?Proves the goal as cega does, and suggests the explicit query to keep in the proof.
      • characteristicPolynomialThe characteristic polynomial det(xI−A) of a square matrix with rational entries, with a theorem about Mathlib’s Matrix.charpoly.
      • ChebyshevTThe Chebyshev polynomial Tn in monomials, proved in every commutative ring, and its exact values at rational points.
      • ChebyshevUThe Chebyshev polynomial Un in monomials, proved in every commutative ring, and its exact values at rational points.
      • chineseRemainderAll solutions of a system of congruences x≡ri(modmi), as one residue modulo the lcm, or a proof that there are none.
      • coefficientGives the coefficient of a power of one variable in a polynomial, with a theorem about Polynomial.coeff.
      • coefficientListGives the coefficients of a polynomial in one variable, constant term first, with a theorem about Polynomial.coeff.
      • collectWrites a polynomial as a sum of powers of one variable, with coefficients in the other variables.
      • continuedFractionThe continued fraction [a0;a1,…,ak] of a rational number, with a theorem that it equals the number.

      D

      • denominatorGives the factored denominator of a rational expression brought to one fraction, with the conditions of the identity.
      • deriveThe derivative of an expression, with the conditions it needs and a theorem about Mathlib’s HasDerivAt.
      • detThe determinant of a square matrix with rational entries, with a theorem about Mathlib’s Matrix.det.
      • divisorsThe divisors of a positive integer below 1012 in increasing order, with a theorem about Mathlib’s Nat.divisors.
      • divisorSigmaThe sum σk(n) of the k-th powers of the divisors of n, with a theorem about Mathlib’s ArithmeticFunction.sigma.
      • dotThe product of two matrices, or of a matrix and a vector, with rational entries and a theorem that it is right.
      • dsolveThe general solution of a linear differential equation, or the solution of an initial-value problem, with a theorem.

      E

      • eigenvalueBoundsDecimal bounds on the smallest and the largest eigenvalue of a symmetric matrix, with a theorem that they hold and are sharp.
      • eigenvaluesThe exact eigenvalues of a square matrix with rational entries, with their multiplicities and a theorem that there are no others.
      • eigenvectorsA basis of each eigenspace of a square matrix with rational entries, with a theorem for every eigenvalue, irrational ones included.
      • encloseAn interval that holds every value of an expression on a box, with upper, absolute and center bounds, all proved.
      • erfProved decimal enclosures of the error function at a rational point, for SymLean’s CAS.erf, defined by the Gaussian integral.
      • eulerPhiEuler’s totient φ(n), the number of residues modulo n coprime to n, with a theorem about Mathlib’s Nat.totient.
      • expandMultiplies out a polynomial, with a theorem that the expansion equals the input for all real values.
      • exponentGives the degree of a polynomial in one variable, with a theorem about Polynomial.natDegree.
      • extendedGCDThe gcd of two natural numbers with Bézout coefficients s and t, and a theorem that as+bt=gcd(a,b).

      F

      • factorFactors a polynomial with rational coefficients, with a theorem that the product equals the input.
      • factorialThe factorial n! of a natural number, with a theorem about Mathlib’s Nat.factorial.
      • factorIntegerThe prime factorization of a natural number n≥2 with a theorem about Nat.primeFactorsList, through the optional Hex library.
      • fibonacciThe Fibonacci number Fn, with F0=0 and F1=1, and a theorem about Mathlib’s Nat.fib.
      • findRootA certified simple root of a function in an interval, to a chosen number of decimals, with a uniqueness theorem.
      • fourierThe Fourier coefficients of a function on [-π, π] as exact functions of n, with a theorem about the integrals.
      • fourierCosThe coefficients of the cosine series on [0, L] as exact functions of n, with a theorem about the integrals.
      • fourierSinThe coefficients of the sine series on [0, L] as exact functions of n, with a theorem about the integrals.
      • fourierTransformThe Fourier transform of a Gaussian in Mathlib’s convention, with a theorem for every frequency.
      • fromContinuedFractionThe rational value of a finite continued fraction [a0;a1,…,ak], with a theorem in ℚ.
      • fullSimplifyTries many checked forms of a real expression and gives the simplest one, with the conditions it needs.

      G

      • gammaThe exact value of the Gamma function at an integer or a half-integer, with a theorem about Mathlib’s Real.Gamma.
      • gradThe gradient of an expression in several variables, with a theorem about Mathlib’s HasGradientAt.
      • groebnerGives the reduced lexicographic Gröbner basis of a polynomial ideal, with theorems about Ideal.span in MvPolynomial.

      H

      • HermiteHThe physicists’ Hermite polynomial Hn in monomials, proved at every real x, and its exact values at rational points.
      • HermiteHeThe probabilists’ Hermite polynomial Hen, Mathlib’s Polynomial.hermite n, in monomials, with its exact values at rational points.

      I

      • integerDigitsThe digits of a natural number in any base b≥2, with a theorem about Mathlib’s Nat.digits.
      • integrateAn antiderivative or a definite integral, checked by differentiation, with a theorem about Mathlib’s interval integral.
      • interpolatingPolynomialGives the polynomial of least degree through given points, with a theorem about its values and degree in ℚ[X].
      • inverseThe inverse of a square matrix with rational entries, or a proof that its determinant is 0.
      • inverseLaplaceA function whose Laplace transform is a given proper rational function, with a theorem about the integral.

      J

      • jacobiSymbolThe Jacobi symbol (a/n) of an integer and an odd natural number, with a theorem about Mathlib’s jacobiSym.

      L

      • laplaceThe Laplace transform of a sum of exponential, trigonometric and hyperbolic terms, with a theorem about the integral.
      • LegendrePThe Legendre polynomial Pn in monomials, proved at every real x, and its exact values at rational points.
      • limitA limit at a rational point, from one side, or at ±∞, with a theorem about Mathlib’s Filter.Tendsto.
      • linearSolveAll solutions of a linear system Ax=b with rational entries, with a theorem that there are no others.

      M

      • matrixPowerThe power Ak of a square matrix with rational entries, for k up to 1000, with a theorem that it is right.
      • matrixRankThe rank of a matrix of any shape with rational entries, with a theorem about Mathlib’s Matrix.rank.
      • maximizeThe maximum of a polynomial on the real line or on an interval, exact or enclosed, with a theorem.
      • memberProves that a polynomial lies in the ideal of given polynomials, and gives it as a combination of them.
      • minimizeThe minimum of a polynomial on the real line or on an interval, exact or enclosed, with a theorem.
      • modularInverseThe inverse of an integer modulo m≥2 in ZMod m, or a proof that there is none.
      • moebiusMuThe Möbius function μ(n) of a positive integer from its prime factorization, through the optional Hex library.

      N

      • NThe first digits of a closed real expression, with a theorem that every printed digit is right.
      • ndsolveCertified enclosures of the solution of a polynomial initial-value problem, with a theorem of existence and uniqueness.
      • nintegrateA definite integral to a fixed number of decimal places, with a theorem that bounds the error.
      • nullSpaceA basis of the null space of a matrix with rational entries, with a theorem that it spans every solution.
      • numeratorGives the numerator of a rational expression brought to one fraction, with the conditions of the identity.

      O

      • OptionsThe settings of cas, cega and #cas: the budget of a call, what a report shows, and the trace classes.
      • oracleAsks SymPy, SageMath, Maxima, PARI/GP, FLINT or Gappa for a proposal, and proves it in Lean or refuses.

      P

      • polynomialGCDGives the monic gcd of two polynomials in one variable, with a theorem about Mathlib’s gcd in ℚ[X].
      • polynomialLCMGives the monic lcm of two nonzero polynomials in one variable, with a theorem about Mathlib’s lcm in ℚ[X].
      • polynomialQuotientGives the quotient of a division of polynomials in one variable, with a theorem about / in ℚ[X].
      • polynomialQuotientRemainderGives the quotient and the remainder of a division of polynomials, with a theorem about / and % in ℚ[X].
      • polynomialRemainderGives the remainder of a division of polynomials in one variable, with a theorem about % in ℚ[X].
      • positiveDefiniteWhether a square matrix with rational entries is positive definite, with a theorem about Mathlib’s Matrix.PosDef either way.
      • powerModThe remainder of ab modulo m by repeated squaring, for exponents with hundreds of digits, with a theorem in ℕ.
      • primeQWhether a natural number is prime, with a proof of Nat.Prime n or its negation, through the optional Hex library.
      • productA finite product in closed form, telescoped or in factorials, with a theorem for every upper bound.

      R

      • reduceA polynomial equation or inequality reduced to an equivalent union of intervals, with a theorem.
      • rootsEvery real or complex root of a polynomial equation, exact or enclosed, with a theorem that the list is complete.
      • rowReduceThe reduced row echelon form of a matrix with rational entries, with the row operations that prove it.
      • rsolveThe general solution of a linear recurrence with constant coefficients, or the sequence with given initial values, with a theorem.

      S

      • seriesA Taylor or Laurent expansion at a rational point, with exact coefficients and a little-o remainder theorem.
      • simplifySimplifies a real expression by checked rewrites, with the conditions under which the result equals it.
      • solveEvery real solution of a polynomial equation or system, with a theorem that the list is complete.
      • sumA finite sum in closed form or by its exact value, or the sum of a series, with a theorem.

      T

      • togetherWrites a rational expression as one fraction in lowest terms, with the conditions under which they agree.
      • traceThe trace of a square matrix with rational entries, with a theorem about Mathlib’s Matrix.trace.
      • transposeThe transpose of a matrix of any shape with rational entries, with a theorem about Mathlib’s Matrix.transpose.
      • trigExpandExpands sines and cosines of sums, differences and double angles, with a theorem about Mathlib’s sine and cosine.

      Z

      • zetaExact values of the Riemann zeta function at even positive and nonpositive integers, with a theorem about Mathlib’s riemannZeta.

      Examples built with Lean 4.35.0. Quoted examples link to their source.
