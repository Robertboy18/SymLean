      Documentation › Differential equations › fourierCos

      Mode of #cas

      fourierCos

      The coefficients of the cosine series on [0, L] as exact functions of n, with a theorem about the integrals.

      #cas [fourierCos x n L] f
      gives the coefficients a0 and an of the cosine series of f on the interval from 0 to L, as exact functions of n for n≥1, and proves the integrals.

      The cosine series on [0,L] is the one that comes up for heat flow in a rod with insulated ends, and fourierCos computes its coefficients. The series is a0/2+∑n≥1ancos(nπx/L). The theorem is about Mathlib’s interval integrals over [0,L], with one statement for a0 and one for an that holds at every n≥1.

      Each term cxjeax of f is integrated by parts, and then sinnπ=0 and cosnπ=(−1)n turn the result into a closed form. For the sine series on the same interval, use fourierSin, and for the full series on [−π,π], use fourier.

      Details and Options

      • For the printed coefficients, the theorem is 2 / L * ∫ x in 0..L, f = a₀ and, for every n : ℕ with 0 < n, 2 / L * ∫ x in 0..L, f * Real.cos (n * Real.pi * x / L) = aₙ. It is printed in simplified form, such as 2 * ∫ x in 0..1, f for L=1.
      • f is a sum of terms c * x ^ j * exp (a * x) with rational a, and π may appear in the coefficients c, as in x * (pi - x). Sines, cosines and other functions are refused.
      • L is a positive rational number or a positive rational multiple of π, such as 2 * pi / 3.
      • When the proof of the coefficients fails, the command is refused.
      • There are no options. In a proof, cas proves the integral behind a single coefficient, as under Applications.

      Properties and Relations (2)

      The cosine series on [0,π] is the Fourier series on [−π,π] of the even extension of f. For the even function x2, the cosine series has the coefficients that fourier gives, π2/4 times those on [0,2] above:

      Out[5]=

      x ∈ [-π, π], n ≥ 1

      Possible Issues (2)

      Sines and cosines are refused on [0,L]. fourier takes them on [−π,π]:

      In[7]:=
      #cas [fourierCos x n pi] (sin x)
      Out[7]=

      Error

      fourierCos: on [0, L] the function must be a sum of terms c x ^ j e ^ (a x)

      The length is a positive rational number or a rational multiple of π, such as 2 * pi / 3:

      In[8]:=
      #cas [fourierCos x n (sqrt 2)] (x)
      Out[8]=

      Error

      fourierCos: L must be a positive rational number or a rational multiple of π
