      Mode of #cas

      continuedFraction

      The continued fraction [a0;a1,…,ak] of a rational number, with a theorem that it equals the number.

      #cas [continuedFraction] q
      gives the continued fraction [a₀; a₁, …, aₖ] of the rational literal q and proves q = a₀ + 1 / (a₁ + 1 / (⋯ + 1 / aₖ)) in ℚ.

      I like continued fractions because they answer a concrete question: how well can a number be approximated by fractions with small denominators? Cutting the expansion off gives the convergents, and each convergent is closer to the number than every fraction with a smaller denominator. continuedFraction computes the expansion of a rational number by recording the quotients of Euclid’s algorithm. The theorem is the identity in ℚ together with the conditions on the terms that make the expansion unique, so it does not just say that the expansion has the value q. It says that this is the continued fraction of q.

      Details and Options

      • q is an exact rational literal, built from natural and decimal literals with -, / and parentheses, such as 415/93, -7/3 or 0.25. A decimal is the exact rational it denotes, so 3.14159 is 314159100000.
      • The theorem is q = a₀ + 1 / (a₁ + 1 / (⋯ + 1 / aₖ)) in ℚ, and for k≥1 also (∀ a ∈ [a₁, …, aₖ], 1 ≤ a) ∧ 2 ≤ aₖ. These conditions make the expansion unique, since […,ak−1,1] has the same value. norm_num proves the identity and decide the conditions.
      • The terms come from Euclid’s algorithm, with a0=⌊q⌋, which may be 0 or negative. An integer n gives [n].
      • An irrational number has an infinite continued fraction and is refused. A decimal approximation from N is a rational literal, with a finite continued fraction that agrees with the infinite one in its first terms.
      • There are no options. fromContinuedFraction goes the other way.

      Applications (2)

      The theorem as a statement in ℚ, with the axioms its proof uses:

      In[6]:=
      theorem continued_fraction_415_93 : (415 / 93 : ℚ) = 4 + 1 / (2 + 1 / (6 + 1 / 7)) := by
  cas

#print axioms continued_fraction_415_93
      Out[6]=

      continued_fraction_415_93 depends on the axioms propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound.
