      Mode of #cas

      fromContinuedFraction

      The rational value of a finite continued fraction [a0;a1,…,ak], with a theorem in ℚ.

      #cas [fromContinuedFraction] [a₀, a₁, …, aₖ]
      gives the rational number a₀ + 1 / (a₁ + 1 / (⋯ + 1 / aₖ)) in lowest terms and proves the identity in ℚ.

      fromContinuedFraction takes the terms of a continued fraction and gives back its value, as Mathematica’s FromContinuedFraction does. It undoes continuedFraction. Where I use it most is for convergents. Cut an expansion off after a few terms and you get a fraction that is closer to the number than every fraction with a smaller denominator, which is why 227 and 355113 approximate π so well. The theorem is the identity in ℚ, and norm_num proves it.

      Details and Options

      • The argument is a list of integer literals [a₀, a₁, …, aₖ] with at least one term. The first term may be zero or negative, and the others are positive.
      • The theorem is q = a₀ + 1 / (a₁ + 1 / (⋯ + 1 / aₖ)) in ℚ, for the printed q in lowest terms.
      • The last term may be 1. Since […,ak−1,1]=[…,ak−1+1], such a list has the value of a shorter one: [1, 1, 1] and [1, 2] are both 32. continuedFraction gives the shorter form, whose last term is at least 2.
      • There are no options.

      Applications (3)

      The theorem as a statement in ℚ, with the axioms its proof uses:

      In[5]:=
      theorem from_continued_fraction_ones : (3 / 2 : ℚ) = 1 + 1 / (1 + 1 / 1) := by
  cas

#print axioms from_continued_fraction_ones
      Out[5]=

      from_continued_fraction_ones depends on the axioms propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound.

      Possible Issues (4)

      A continued fraction has at least the term a0:

      In[13]:=
      #cas [fromContinuedFraction] []
      Out[13]=

      Error

      fromContinuedFraction: the list is empty

      Only the first term may be zero or negative:

      In[14]:=
      #cas [fromContinuedFraction] [1, 0, 2]
      Out[14]=

      Error

      fromContinuedFraction: the terms after the first must be positive

      The terms are integers:

      In[15]:=
      #cas [fromContinuedFraction] [1, 1/2]
      Out[15]=

      Error

      fromContinuedFraction: expected integers

      The terms go in a list, in square brackets:

      In[16]:=
      #cas [fromContinuedFraction] (4, 2, 6, 7)
      Out[16]=

      Error

      fromContinuedFraction: expected a list `[a₀, a₁, …]`
