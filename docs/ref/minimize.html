      Documentation › Calculus › minimize

      Mode of #cas

      minimize

      The minimum of a polynomial on the real line or on an interval, exact or enclosed, with a theorem.

      #cas [minimize x] p
      gives the global minimum of the polynomial p, exactly when a rational point attains it and otherwise as a certified enclosure, and proves that no value of p is smaller.
      #cas [minimize x in [a, b]] p
      gives the minimum of p on the closed interval [a,b] with rational endpoints a<b, with a point of the interval that attains it or comes within the enclosure.
      #cas (digits := d) [minimize x] p
      the same with an enclosure of width at most 2·10−d, for d from 1 to 6. The interval form takes d too.

      minimize looks for the smallest value of a polynomial and proves that nothing goes below it. When a rational point attains the minimum, the answer is exact, and the theorem says m≤p(x) for every real x, and p(x0)=m.

      Often the minimizer is irrational, as for x4−x, whose minimizer is 4−1/3. Then the answer is an enclosure [lo,hi] of the minimum, and the theorem says lo≤p(x) for every x, and p(x1)≤hi at a rational point x1 near the minimizer. What it bounds is the value of the minimum, not the position of the minimizer.

      Details and Options

      • p is a polynomial in x with rational coefficients and no other variable.
      • The exact theorem is (∀x,m≤p)∧p(x0)=m, and the enclosure theorem is (∀x,lo≤p)∧p(x1)≤hi. Lean writes the value at the point as (fun x ↦ p) x₀.
      • The candidates are the real roots of the derivative p′. The bound comes from the sign certificate that cas uses for p−m≥0, and the value at the point from norm_num.
      • A polynomial of odd degree, or of even degree with a negative leading coefficient, is unbounded below and is refused. On a closed interval every polynomial has a minimum.
      • On [a,b] the theorem is (∀x∈[a,b],m≤p)∧x0∈[a,b]∧p(x0)=m, stated with Set.Icc a b, or its enclosure form with a witness x1 in the interval.
      • (digits := d) makes the enclosure at most 2·10−d wide, for d from 1 to 6, by default 3. More digits make the sign certificate slower.
      • maximize is minimize of −p. In a proof, cas proves the bound ∀x,m≤p, and cega proves bounds on an interval for functions other than polynomials.

      Applications (2)

      The bound in a proof. cas proves it, and only the three standard axioms are used:

      In[11]:=
      theorem minimize_quadratic : ∀ x : ℝ, 3 ≤ x ^ 2 - 2 * x + 4 := by
  cas

#print axioms minimize_quadratic
      Out[11]=

      minimize_quadratic depends on the axioms propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound.
