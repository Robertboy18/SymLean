      Function of #cas

      divisorSigma

      The sum σk(n) of the k-th powers of the divisors of n, with a theorem about Mathlib’s ArithmeticFunction.sigma.

      #cas (divisorSigma k n)
      gives the sum σk(n) of the k-th powers of the divisors of the positive natural number n and proves ArithmeticFunction.sigma k n = s.

      divisorSigma computes the divisor functions: σ0(n) counts the divisors of n, and σ1(n) adds them up. The theorem is about Mathlib’s ArithmeticFunction.sigma. What I like about the proof is that the kernel checks the divisors with a counting certificate and no factorization at all, so how hard n is to factor never comes into it.

      Details and Options

      • k and n are natural numbers, literals or closed integer expressions, with 1≤n<1012 and k<232, the exponents for which the kernel evaluates the powers dk of the certificate.
      • The theorem is ArithmeticFunction.sigma k n = s, by CAS.NumberTheory.sigma_eq_of_cert. The kernel checks the list of divisors of n with a counting certificate, ⌊n⌋ divisions and no factorization, and sums their k-th powers.
      • The value has at most 10000 digits, the most that a call computes.
      • divisors prints the list of divisors with the same certificate.
      • The call is evaluated when it is the whole input of #cas.
      • There are no options.

      Examples (7)#

      Basic Examples (1)

      The sum of the divisors of 360. σ1 is multiplicative, and σ1(pe)=1+p+⋯+pe on a prime power, so the sum is σ1(23)σ1(32)σ1(5)=15·13·6=1170:

      In[1]:=
      #cas (divisorSigma 1 360)
      Out[1]=

      divisorSigma 1

      1170

      Applications (1)

      In a proof, cas proves the equation:

      In[4]:=
      theorem sigma_two_twelve : ArithmeticFunction.sigma 2 12 = 210 := by
  cas

#print axioms sigma_two_twelve
      Out[4]=

      sigma_two_twelve depends on the axioms propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound.

      Possible Issues (2)

      Every natural number divides 0, and Mathlib sets ArithmeticFunction.sigma k 0 = 0. The call takes positive n only:

      In[6]:=
      #cas (divisorSigma 1 0)
      Out[6]=

      Error

      divisorSigma: expected a positive natural number

      The value has at most 10000 digits. The prime 999999937 has σ4000=1+9999999374000, with 36000 digits:

      In[7]:=
      #cas (divisorSigma 4000 999999937)
      Out[7]=

      Error

      divisorSigma: the value has more than 10000 digits, the most that a call computes
