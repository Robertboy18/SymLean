      Mode of #cas

      characteristicPolynomial

      The characteristic polynomial det(xI−A) of a square matrix with rational entries, with a theorem about Mathlib’s Matrix.charpoly.

      #cas [characteristicPolynomial] A
      gives the characteristic polynomial det(xI−A) of the square matrix A with rational entries, and proves A.charpoly = p in ℚ[X].

      The characteristic polynomial holds a lot of a matrix at once: its roots are the eigenvalues, and the trace and the determinant can be read off its coefficients. It also appears in the Cayley-Hamilton theorem and in formulas for linear recurrences. characteristicPolynomial computes it, with a theorem about Mathlib’s Matrix.charpoly, the monic polynomial det(XI−A). If you are coming from Mathematica, watch the sign. Mathematica’s CharacteristicPolynomial is det(A−xI), which is (−1)n times this one.

      Details and Options

      • A is a square matrix literal !![a, b; c, d] or a list of rows [[a, b], [c, d]]. The entries are closed rational expressions such as -3/2, 0.25 or 1/10^15.
      • The theorem is A.charpoly = p in ℚ[X], with p written in Polynomial.X. The proof compares both sides at every point t (Polynomial.funext): Matrix.eval_charpoly turns the value of A.charpoly at t into the determinant of tI−A, which norm_det evaluates.
      • For an n×n matrix the polynomial is monic of degree n. The coefficient of xn−1 is −trA and the constant term is (−1)ndetA.
      • The tactic cas proves goals Matrix.charpoly A = p for matrix literals over ℚ.

      Applications (3)

      The polynomial as a theorem, with the axioms its proof uses:

      In[6]:=
      theorem charpoly_two :
    Matrix.charpoly !![(1 : ℚ), 2; 3, 4] = Polynomial.X ^ 2 - 5 * Polynomial.X - 2 := by
  cas

#print axioms charpoly_two
      Out[6]=

      charpoly_two depends on the axioms propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound.

      With the theorem above, Mathlib’s Cayley-Hamilton theorem Matrix.aeval_self_charpoly says that the matrix is a root of the printed polynomial, A2−5A−2I=0:

      In[7]:=
      example : Polynomial.aeval !![(1 : ℚ), 2; 3, 4]
    (Polynomial.X ^ 2 - 5 * Polynomial.X - 2 : Polynomial ℚ) = 0 := by
  rw [← charpoly_two]
  exact Matrix.aeval_self_charpoly _
      Out[7]=
