      Documentation › Algebra › together

      Mode of #cas

      together

      Writes a rational expression as one fraction in lowest terms, with the conditions under which they agree.

      #cas [together] e
      writes the rational expression e as one fraction in lowest terms and gives the conditions under which the fraction equals e.

      together puts a sum of fractions over one denominator, the way you would by hand. The answer comes with a theorem, and the conditions of the theorem come from the divisors of the input.

      On paper we rarely write those conditions down, but Lean needs them, because its division is total: 1/0=0. At x=0 the sum 1/x+1/y is 1/y in Lean, while (x+y)/(xy) is y/0=0. So the identity needs x≠0 and y≠0, and the theorem has them as hypotheses.

      Details and Options

      • The theorem has the form ∀x1,…,xn,d1≠0→…→dk≠0→e=N/D about Mathlib’s real division, where d1,…,dk are the divisors of e as written. It is proved by field_simp and ring.
      • The input is built from real variables and rational numbers by +, -, *, / and natural powers, and every divisor is a polynomial.
      • The denominator D is a product of powers of pairwise coprime polynomials obtained from the divisors by gcds, so it stays factored as far as the input factors it. Each factor of D divides one of the di, which ring shows. The numerator N is expanded.
      • A divisor that is a number gives no condition.
      • numerator and denominator print N and D, with the same theorem. cancel cancels inside each summand without a common denominator, and apart goes the other way.
      • The tactic cas proves these identities, with the conditions as hypotheses.

      Applications (2)

      The first example as a theorem, with the conditions as hypotheses. Only the three standard axioms are used:

      In[5]:=
      theorem reciprocals_together (x y : ℝ) (hx : x ≠ 0) (hy : y ≠ 0) :
    1 / x + 1 / y = (x + y) / (x * y) := by
  cas

#print axioms reciprocals_together
      Out[5]=

      reciprocals_together depends on the axioms propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound.

      Without the conditions the identity is false in Lean. At x=0 and y=1 the left side is 1 and the right side is 1/0=0:

      In[6]:=
      example : ¬ ∀ x y : ℝ, 1 / x + 1 / y = (x + y) / (x * y) := by
  intro h
  have := h 0 1
  norm_num at this
      Out[6]=

      ✓ Lean accepts the proof.

      Possible Issues (3)

      The conditions are the divisors as written. Here (xy+x−x)2 is x2y2, and the condition keeps the form of the input:

      In[11]:=
      #cas [together] (1/((x*y + x - x)^2) + 1)
      Out[11]=

      common denominator

      x2y2+1x2y2
      (x^2*y^2 + 1)/(x^2*y^2)
      Input (rational function)
      (1/((x*y + x - x)^2) + 1)
      Conditions
      (xy+x−x)2≠0

      ✓ Checked by Lean’s kernel: Identity with the common-denominator form

      ∀ (x y : ℝ),
  (x * y + x - x) ^ 2 ≠ 0 → 1 / (x * y + x - x) ^ 2 + 1 = (x ^ 2 * y ^ 2 + 1) / (x ^ 2 * y ^ 2)
      The certificate: 1 checked step

      1. Identity with the common-denominator form

        ∀ (x y : ℝ),
  (x * y + x - x) ^ 2 ≠ 0 → 1 / (x * y + x - x) ^ 2 + 1 = (x ^ 2 * y ^ 2 + 1) / (x ^ 2 * y ^ 2)

        Checked by Lean’s kernel

      A divisor must be a polynomial. Multiply the inner fraction out first. For x≠0 the expression is x/(x+1):

      In[12]:=
      #cas [together] (1/(1 + 1/x))
      Out[12]=

      Error

      together: a divisor is itself a quotient; write the expression with polynomial divisors

      Functions other than the field operations are refused. simplify and fullSimplify accept them:

      In[13]:=
      #cas [together] (sin x / x)
      Out[13]=

      Error

      together: the expression is not a rational function: it has a function other than the field operations and natural powers
