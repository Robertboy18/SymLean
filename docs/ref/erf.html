      Function of #cas

      erf

      Proved decimal enclosures of the error function at a rational point, for SymLean’s CAS.erf, defined by the Gaussian integral.

      #cas [N d] (erf q)
      proves a decimal enclosure of width at most 10−d of erfq at a rational q, and prints it.

      erf gives certified decimals for the error function. The Mathlib we build on doesn’t have an error function, so erf is our own CAS.erf, defined by the Gaussian integral

      erfx=2π∫0xe−t2dt.

      What you get back is a decimal interval of width at most 10−d and a theorem that erfq lies in it. The call only ever computes enclosures, and for the error function that’s usually what I want anyway: a number I can use in a proof, as accurate as I asked for.

      Details and Options

      • q is a rational number with |q|≤10 and a denominator of at most 1000, and 1≤d≤30.
      • The theorem is a ≤ CAS.erf q ∧ CAS.erf q ≤ b ∧ b - a ≤ 1 / 10 ^ d for the printed interval [a,b], by CAS.erfCheck_sound.
      • The kernel checks a partial sum of the Taylor series with a bound on its tail, and an interval for π from Machin’s formula. The Output line says how many terms of the series the check used.
      • The call computes enclosures only, so #cas (erf q) is refused and points to #cas [N d] (erf q). The library proves the exact value CAS.erf_zero : CAS.erf 0 = 0.
      • There are no options.

      Applications (1)

      A proof can take an enclosure as a hypothesis, here a coarser one than the printed interval:

      In[6]:=
      theorem erf_one_pos (h : 21 / 25 ≤ CAS.erf 1 ∧ CAS.erf 1 ≤ 17 / 20) : 0 < CAS.erf 1 := by
  linarith [h.1]

#print axioms erf_one_pos
      Out[6]=

      erf_one_pos depends on the axioms propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound.

      Possible Issues (3)

      #cas gives no exact value of erf, and the message points to [N d]:

      In[9]:=
      #cas (erf 1)
      Out[9]=

      Error

      `#cas` computes no exact value of `erf x`; `#cas [N d] (erf x)` gives its digits

      The point is a rational with |q|≤10 and a denominator of at most 1000. The series converges slowly far from 0, and the bound keeps the kernel’s partial sum short:

      In[10]:=
      #cas [N 10] (erf 11)
      Out[10]=

      Error

      erf accepts rational points with |q| ≤ 10 and a denominator of at most 1000

      The call gives at most 30 digits:

      In[11]:=
      #cas [N 31] (erf 1)
      Out[11]=

      Error

      erf accepts at most 30 digits
