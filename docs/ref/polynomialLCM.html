      Documentation › Polynomials › polynomialLCM

      Mode of #cas

      polynomialLCM

      Gives the monic lcm of two nonzero polynomials in one variable, with a theorem about Mathlib’s lcm in ℚ[X].

      #cas [polynomialLCM] (p, q)
      gives the monic least common multiple l of the nonzero polynomials p and q and proves lcm p q = l in Mathlib’s ℚ[X].

      You meet a polynomial lcm every time you put fractions over a common denominator. polynomialLCM computes it in ℚ[X], and the theorem is about Mathlib’s lcm in ℚ[X], the normalized lcm, which is monic.

      The proof goes through the gcd. Both polynomials divide l with explicit cofactors, the gcd g comes with the certificate of polynomialGCD, and g·l divides pq. From that, Mathlib’s gcd_mul_lcm shows that l divides lcm p q.

      Details and Options

      • The theorem is lcm p q = l in ℚ[X], with the variable read as Polynomial.X. The two inputs have the same single variable.
      • The lcm is monic, so it can have fractions as coefficients when the inputs have integer ones.
      • The inputs are nonzero polynomials with rational coefficients in one variable. Mathlib sets lcm 0 p = 0, and a zero input is refused.
      • The product of the gcd and the lcm is pq divided by the leading coefficients of p and q.
      • The tactic cas proves lcm p q = l goals in ℚ[X], and it refuses a wrong lcm with the right one in its message.

      Applications (2)

      The first example as a theorem about Mathlib’s lcm. Only the three standard axioms are used:

      In[4]:=
      open Polynomial in
theorem lcm_of_quadratics :
    lcm (X ^ 2 - 1 : ℚ[X]) (X ^ 2 - 2 * X + 1) = X ^ 3 - X ^ 2 - X + 1 := by
  cas

#print axioms lcm_of_quadratics
      Out[4]=

      lcm_of_quadratics depends on the axioms propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound.

      Properties and Relations (1)

      polynomialGCD gives the gcd x−1 of the first example. Its product with the lcm is (x2−1)(x−1)2:

      Possible Issues (2)

      Mathlib’s lcm 0 p is 0, and a zero input is refused:

      In[7]:=
      #cas [polynomialLCM] (0, x)
      Out[7]=

      Error

      polynomialLCM: the polynomials must be nonzero (lcm 0 p = 0)

      The product (x2−1)(x−1)=x3−x2−x+1 is a common multiple of x2−1 and x−1, but not the least one. The tactic cas refuses it, with the lcm in its message:

      In[8]:=
      open Polynomial in
example : lcm (X ^ 2 - 1 : ℚ[X]) (X - 1) = X ^ 3 - X ^ 2 - X + 1 := by
  cas
      Out[8]=

      Error

      cas: the lcm is X^2 - 1, not X^3 - X^2 - X + 1
