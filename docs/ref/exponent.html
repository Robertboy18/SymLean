      Documentation › Polynomials › exponent

      Mode of #cas

      exponent

      Gives the degree of a polynomial in one variable, with a theorem about Polynomial.natDegree.

      #cas [exponent x] e
      gives the degree of the polynomial e in x and proves it about Mathlib’s Polynomial.natDegree.

      The degree of a polynomial isn’t always the one you see on the page, because terms can cancel. exponent reads the degree off the expanded form, after the cancellations, so (x+1)3−x3 has degree 2. The theorem is P.natDegree = d about Mathlib’s polynomials.

      Parameters make this more delicate than it looks. When the leading coefficient involves the other variables it could be zero, so the theorem assumes that it isn’t. For ax2+x+1 the theorem is ∀ a : ℝ, a ≠ 0 → (C a * X ^ 2 + X + 1).natDegree = 2, and the report prints the condition a≠0. I would much rather see that condition spelled out than get a degree that is wrong for one value of a.

      Details and Options

      • The theorem is P.natDegree = d, where P is e read as a polynomial as for coefficient: in ℚ[X], or in ℝ[X] for all values of the other variables, which enter as constants C y.
      • When the leading coefficient c involves the other variables, the statement is c≠0→ P.natDegree = d.
      • The degree is that of the expanded polynomial, so terms that cancel do not count.
      • The zero polynomial is refused. Mathlib sets natDegree 0 = 0 and degree 0 = ⊥.
      • The input is a polynomial with rational coefficients, as for collect. A quotient by a term with a variable is refused.
      • The tactic cas proves natDegree goals in ℚ[X], and it refuses a wrong degree with the right one in its message.

      Applications (1)

      The first example as a theorem about Polynomial.natDegree. Only the three standard axioms are used:

      In[4]:=
      open Polynomial in
theorem degree_of_difference : ((X + 1) ^ 3 - X ^ 3 : ℚ[X]).natDegree = 2 := by
  cas

#print axioms degree_of_difference
      Out[4]=

      degree_of_difference depends on the axioms propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound.

      Possible Issues (2)

      The zero polynomial is refused:

      In[9]:=
      #cas [exponent x] (0)
      Out[9]=

      Error

      exponent: the polynomial is zero

      The tactic cas refuses a wrong degree and gives the right one:

      In[10]:=
      open Polynomial in
example : (X ^ 3 + 1 : ℚ[X]).natDegree = 2 := by
  cas
      Out[10]=

      Error

      cas: the degree is 3, not 2
