      Documentation › Number theory › divisors

      Mode of #cas

      divisors

      The divisors of a positive integer below 1012 in increasing order, with a theorem about Mathlib’s Nat.divisors.

      #cas [divisors] n
      lists the divisors of the positive integer n in increasing order, and proves Nat.divisors n = {d₁, …, dₖ}.

      divisors lists the divisors themselves, which is what you want for checking a perfect number, an amicable pair or a claim about Nat.divisors n. The proof does not factor n. The kernel pairs each divisor d≤n with its cofactor n/d and counts the small ones with ⌊n⌋ divisions, so the time depends on the size of n and not on its factors.

      Details and Options

      • n is a natural number with 1≤n<1012: a literal such as 360, or a closed integer expression such as (2^10 - 1).
      • The theorem is Nat.divisors n = {d₁, …, dₖ}, an equation in Finset ℕ about Mathlib’s Nat.divisors.
      • The proof is a counting certificate (CAS.NumberTheory.divisors_eq_of_cert). With m=⌊n⌋, the kernel checks that the listed divisors up to m divide n and are as many as the d≤m with d∣n, and that the others are their cofactors n/d. Below 1012 this takes at most a million divisions.
      • The report prints the divisors in increasing order. A number below 1012 has at most 6720 of them.
      • There are no options. divisorSigma gives the number and the sums of powers of the divisors from the same certificate, without the list.

      The theorem, with the axioms its proof uses:

      In[6]:=
      theorem divisors_twelve : Nat.divisors 12 = {1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12} := by

#print axioms divisors_twelve
      Out[6]=

      divisors_twelve depends on the axioms propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound.

      Possible Issues (2)

      Mathlib’s Nat.divisors 0 is the empty set, and 0 is refused:

      In[12]:=
      #cas [divisors] 0
      Out[12]=

      Error

      divisors: expected a positive natural number

      The divisors are certified for n<1012 only:

      In[13]:=
      #cas [divisors] (10^12)
      Out[13]=

      Error

      divisors: the divisors are certified for n < 10^12; `10^12` is beyond that bound
