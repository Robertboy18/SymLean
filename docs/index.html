      Documentation

      Every mode of #cas, every tactic and every option, with examples that Lean compiled when this site was built.

      The guides go one area at a time, and each one lists its modes with a line on what every mode does. Each mode then has a page of its own: what you write, what it means, and examples from the simplest case up to the ones that fail, with the messages Lean really prints. I wanted the failures in there, because they show what a mode promises as clearly as the successes do.

      If this is your first time, start with Getting started. If you want to know how far you can trust an answer before you build on it, read What you trust.

      • Why SymLean

        What can go wrong in the output of a computer algebra system, mistakes on record from Mathematica to SymPy, Sage, Maxima, FLINT and PARI/GP, and how proving the answer catches them.

      • Algebra

        Simplifying, expanding, factoring and combining fractions, each with a theorem about real numbers and its conditions.

        simplify fullSimplify expand factor collect trigExpand together cancel and 3 more

      • Polynomials

        Coefficients, degrees, division, gcds, interpolation and Gröbner bases, with theorems about Mathlib’s Polynomial and MvPolynomial.

        coefficient coefficientList exponent polynomialGCD polynomialLCM polynomialQuotient polynomialRemainder polynomialQuotientRemainder and 3 more

      • Calculus

        Derivatives, gradients, antiderivatives, definite integrals and extrema, each with a theorem about Mathlib’s definitions.

        derive grad integrate nintegrate cas [integrate primitive := F] maximize minimize

      • Series and limits

        Taylor and Laurent expansions with a little-o remainder, and limits at a point, from one side or at ±∞.

        series limit

      • Equations

        Polynomial equations, systems and inequalities solved completely, and certified roots of other equations in an interval.

        solve reduce roots findRoot

      • Sums and recurrences

        Finite sums and products in closed form, sums of series, and linear recurrences, each with a theorem.

        sum product rsolve

      • Special functions

        Gamma and zeta values, Bernoulli numbers, the error function, Bessel functions and the classical orthogonal polynomials, each with its theorem.

        gamma zeta BernoulliB erf BesselJ LegendreP ChebyshevT ChebyshevU and 2 more

      • Number theory

        Primes, factorizations, divisors, congruences, digits and continued fractions, each with a theorem about Mathlib’s definitions.

        primeQ factorInteger moebiusMu divisors divisorSigma eulerPhi powerMod modularInverse and 9 more

      • Linear algebra

        Determinants, inverses, ranks, linear systems and eigenvalues of matrices with rational entries, each with a theorem about Mathlib’s Matrix.

        dot matrixPower transpose trace det inverse matrixRank rowReduce and 7 more

      • Differential equations

        Linear differential equations solved exactly, certified initial-value problems, Laplace and Fourier transforms, and Fourier series.

        dsolve ndsolve laplace inverseLaplace fourierTransform fourier fourierCos fourierSin

      • Numerics

        Digits, ranges, integrals, roots and eigenvalue bounds, each a proved enclosure, and the tactic cega that proves numerical bounds.

        N enclose maximize minimize nintegrate findRoot ndsolve eigenvalueBounds and 4 more

      • Certificates

        The statements behind the numerics: enclosures, bounds and approximations on a box, the carriers that hold them, and the tactics that prove and reuse them.

        cega enclose cega using cega? Options

      • Tactics

        The tactics: cas for identities, calculus and limits, cega for numerical bounds, cas_nf for normal forms, and the options that tune them.

        cas cas using cega cega using cas? cega? cas_nf Options

      • Oracles

        SymPy, SageMath, Maxima, PARI/GP, FLINT and Gappa as untrusted search, with every proposal proved in Lean or refused.

        oracle cas cas [integrate primitive := F] Options

      • Hex

        The optional routes through Hex: irreducibility, determinants and primes in cas, factors over the rationals with every one certified, and primality.

        cas factor apart product eigenvalues eigenvectors primeQ moebiusMu and 4 more

      • The library

        The public imports, how the modules are organized, the registries that extend the tactics, and how to check a change.
